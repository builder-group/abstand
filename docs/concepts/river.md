# The River

The river is the metaphor at the core of Abstand.

Your attention is like a river. It carries a fixed amount of energy. Concentrated, it moves with power and travels far. Branched across too many things: tabs, apps, notifications, context switches. It spreads thin. You feel busy. Nothing moves.

Abstand builds the banks. Not to stop the river, but to give it direction. A river without banks does not disappear, it just loses its shape and goes nowhere. Banks do not fight the water. They focus it.

This is why blocking works better than willpower. Willpower tries to hold the water back. Banks just redirect it. You are not fighting your attention. You are shaping where it goes.

**Block** builds hard banks: walls that keep the river in its channel for the full duration.

**Pulse** creates rhythm: regular moments where the river pauses and recharges before continuing.

When attention tries to escape to a blocked app or website, it branches. The branch goes nowhere. The main channel keeps flowing.
