# Abstand

Most people know what they want to work on. The problem is not a lack of discipline. It is that digital environments pull at attention constantly, and every small detour adds up. By the end of the day, the work that mattered did not happen.

An Abstand is the active experience of stepping back on purpose. It begins when an Intention fires and lasts until the end condition is met or the user stops it early. The word is German for distance, and that is exactly what it creates: intentional distance from the things pulling at your attention.

"I'm taking an Abstand." "I took a 3-hour Abstand this morning." This is the moment the app is built around.

## How it is enforced

An Abstand is always created by an Intention. The Intention defines when it fires and which behavior enforces the distance:

- **Block**: a hard wall. The river has banks. Distractions are inaccessible.
- **Break**: a rhythm of breaks. The river pauses and recharges at intervals.

The Abstand is the same in both cases: intentional distance. The behavior is the mechanism that creates it.

## Portage

Ending an Abstand early is called Portage. There is no failure state. The user chose to stop, and that is treated as a valid decision.
